timing directly. The actual song timeline is the largest thing on screen.

OWN-WORLD: True-black-flat app sheet. Near-black neutral surfaces,
Inter for compact labels, tabular times, one lime energy line, pale vocal regions
and amber manual markers. Conventional buttons, selects, table and native player.

add/remove markers and save a correction revision. Unknown vocals and confidence
are explicit. No invented listening judgments.

history beside it. On narrow screens the track picker and editor stack.
