Continue step 3 of the saved context-aware workflow.

1. Generate at most twelve deterministic candidates per ordered pair using the
   effective, revisioned timelines. Vary cue anchors AND 32/16/8-bar blend or
   phrase-cut recipe. Record source/model/correction provenance and evidence.
2. Score local energy trend, bass/chroma/embedding contrast, vocal activity,
   rhythm regularity and boundary support. Protect rises and require additional
   phrase/boundary evidence for cuts; unknown features remain unknown. This is a
   conservative heuristic baseline, not a trained contextual model.
3. Screen source bounds, stretch limits, phrase geometry and energy collapse.
   Independently prepare and waveform-validate proposals before final selection.
   Allow only bounded phase correction; retain requested and applied settings.
   Failed technical checks disqualify the candidate rather than change its recipe.
4. Persist immutable candidate snapshots and append-only validation/audition
   records. Render the best two distinct valid alternatives at a common
   handoff timestamp and duration, with matched audition loudness and headroom.
   Add a CLI calling the shared services, retaining all rejection reasons.
5. Integrate validated contextual choices into an opt-in full-set planning path,
   preserving every input and checking adjacent excerpt/overlap geometry. Keep
   the conservative existing planner available and report fallback joins.
6. Test buildup protection, vocal collisions, unknown/incorrect boundaries,
   short sources, tempo outliers, revision provenance, all four rendered recipes,
   waveform drift/clipping/silence/tails, and complete membership. Analyze the
   second track of the previously used Move/Say What pair and render a real
   comparison. No fabricated listening feedback or model promotion.
